Privacy Policy

How Drywall Painter Pro collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website or contact us.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Drywall Painter Pro (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates drywallpainterpro.com and provides painting, drywall, flooring, TV mounting, and handyman services in the Chicago area. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle and why.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to visitors of our website, people who call or text us, and anyone who submits an estimate request by email or through our contact form.

Information you give us

When you request an estimate or contact us, you may provide:

  • Name and phone number
  • Email address
  • City or service location
  • Project details, photos you choose to send, and preferred timing

We use this information only to respond to your request, prepare quotes, schedule work, and communicate about jobs in progress. We do not sell your contact details to lead brokers or marketing lists.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and DNS providers may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and timestamps. This helps keep the site secure and diagnose outages. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles.

Cookies and local storage

Our site may store a simple theme preference (light/dark) in your browser’s local storage so the appearance stays consistent when you return. We do not currently run advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. If we add analytics later, we will update this page.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — pages may load fonts from Google’s servers, which can see your IP address.
  • Email / phone — if you use a mailto link or call/text us, that communication goes through your own carrier or email provider.
  • Hosting — the site is hosted through our web host / GitHub Pages infrastructure, which processes standard server logs.

How long we keep information

Estimate and job-related messages are kept as long as needed to serve you and for ordinary business records (scheduling, warranties, accounting). You can ask us to delete contact details that are no longer needed for an active or recent job.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate for a small local service business. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so please avoid sending sensitive financial documents through the public contact form unless we specifically ask for them through a secure channel.

Children’s privacy

Our services are directed to homeowners and property managers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can request a copy of the contact information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate details.
  • You can ask us to delete estimate-request data that is not required for an active job or legal recordkeeping.
  • You can opt out of non-essential marketing messages at any time (we primarily use contact info for job-related communication).

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after updates means you accept the revised policy.
